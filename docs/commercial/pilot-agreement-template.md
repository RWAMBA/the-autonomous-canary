# CanaryGuard Founding Pilot Agreement

This agreement is between **Valerie Rwamba Munyi trading as NextEdge Analytics** (Provider) and **[Customer legal name]** (Customer). It becomes effective on the last signature date.

## 1 Scope

Provider will conduct a 14-day CanaryGuard release-governance pilot for one GitHub repository identified in the order details. The work includes a release-workflow assessment, least-privilege GitHub App installation, configuration of agreed deterministic release rules, four validation scenarios, one evidence report and one closeout review.

The pilot does not include custom feature development, continuous production operations, regulatory certification, legal advice, penetration testing, incident response, source-code remediation, cloud-infrastructure administration or work on repositories not listed in the order details.

## 2 Customer responsibilities

Customer will appoint a technical owner and an installation approver, provide timely access to the pilot repository, maintain its own backups and deployment controls, and avoid submitting credentials, private keys, production passwords, unrestricted raw logs or other prohibited content. Customer retains authority over deployments and production changes.

## 3 Access and security

Customer authorizes the minimum GitHub App permissions described in the pilot brief for the pilot repository. Provider will not request repository contents write access. Each party will use reasonable administrative and technical controls for credentials under its control and will notify the other party promptly of a confirmed security incident affecting pilot data.

## 4 Confidentiality

Each party will protect non-public information received from the other party and use it only to perform or evaluate the pilot. Confidential information excludes information that is public without breach, already lawfully known, independently developed, or lawfully received from another source. A party may disclose information when legally required after giving notice where permitted.

## 5 Data handling

The public intake form collects bounded business contact and release-challenge information. CanaryGuard excludes source archives, credentials, private keys, production passwords, unrestricted raw logs, prompts and raw model output from the documented evidence path. Lead records receive a 180-day retention deadline unless the parties agree another lawful basis or period in writing. Customer remains responsible for determining whether its use of the pilot satisfies its legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

## 6 Fees and payment

The fixed pilot fee is **USD 750**. Customer will pay **50 percent before kickoff and 50 percent on delivery of the closeout report**, unless the order details state another schedule. Fees are non-refundable for work already performed. Any taxes or payment-processing charges payable by Customer will be stated on the invoice or payment request where applicable.

## 7 Acceptance and success criteria

The parties will record the baseline, agreed release rules and success criteria at kickoff. Provider will present the pilot evidence at closeout. Customer will identify any material failure to deliver the written scope within five business days after closeout. Provider will make a reasonable correction limited to the agreed scope.

## 8 Intellectual property

Each party retains its pre-existing intellectual property. CanaryGuard, its software, methods, templates and improvements remain Provider property. Customer retains its repositories, software, data and trademarks. Payment grants Customer no source-code ownership or open-source licence. Any continuing service or broader licence requires a separate written agreement.

## 9 Warranties and limitation

Provider will perform the pilot with reasonable care and skill. CanaryGuard supplies release-governance evidence and policy decisions but does not guarantee defect-free software, uninterrupted service, regulatory compliance, prevention of every failed deployment or a specific commercial result. To the extent permitted by applicable law, neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Provider's aggregate liability under this pilot will not exceed the fees paid under this agreement, except where applicable law does not permit that limitation.

## 10 Termination

Either party may terminate for a material breach not corrected within five business days after written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for unlawful use, a material security risk or repeated failure to provide required access. On termination, Customer will pay for work completed and authorized non-cancellable costs. Provider will revoke pilot access and handle retained data under the agreed retention terms.

## 11 Publicity

Provider will not publish Customer's name, logo, testimonial, metrics or case study without Customer's prior written approval.

## 12 General terms

The parties will state the governing law and dispute forum in the order details before signature. Changes must be in writing and accepted by both parties. If one provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue. Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted where legally effective.
